# Style bible

## Frame and composition
- Reference composition is 16:9 with generous negative space and a floor line near 78% of frame height.
- The hero is often small within a large dark space; close-ups are reserved for emotional peaks.
- Use film grain, a soft vignette, and fades from black. Reserve hard cuts for impact beats.
- Recompose for portrait output; do not rely on a centre crop for important subjects or text.

## Characters
- **Hero:** cream (#ECE5D4) pictogram with a round head, rounded torso, thick round-ended limbs, and simple facial features. A red headband marks the post-turn version.
- **Enemy:** near-black silhouette, slightly taller than the hero, with a red outer glow and curved red eye slits. It stands behind the hero or appears in a mirror, and gradually shrinks.
- Family stories may add simple coloured clothing and hair while keeping the visual language minimal.

## Colour script
| Act | Palette | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Loop / enemy | night, cold | charcoal, grey, moonlit window |
| Rock bottom | black + spotlight | isolate the hero |
| Turn | night with flash | red anchor detail |
| Climb | night → warm → sunset | streetlights and amber glow |
| End | dawn, sunset | return to the opening window |

Red is reserved for the enemy, anchor details, headband, and punch line.

## Type
- Captions are small, white, centred near the top third, and ideally no more than 12 words.
- Whispers are lowercase red typewriter text near the enemy.
- Day labels are small; the turning point can use a large centred DAY 1.
- Title: one heavy word with a hand-drawn red underline.
- Poppins font files are included in the source archive; check their licence before redistribution.

## Pacing and sound
- Opening and rock bottom: slow, approximately 4–6 seconds per scene.
- Montage: faster, approximately 2.5–4 seconds per scene.
- Title: approximately 6–8 seconds.
- Sound palette: low minor drone, near-silence at rock bottom, alarm cue at the turn, hopeful piano during the climb, and restrained impact cues.
- Keep narration clear; avoid music swells under key lines.
