## Production observations

- Repeated sets and day labels make time passing legible without extensive exposition.
- The enemy's language escalates from comfort to doubt, identity attack, isolation, and a final challenge.
- The emotional turn is strongest when tied to a concrete action or conversation.
- The shadow is not necessarily eliminated; it becomes smaller or less influential.
- Do not copy the reference films' exact scripts, titles, dialogue, or characters.
